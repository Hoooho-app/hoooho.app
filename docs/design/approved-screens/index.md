## Brand breakthrough samples

Approval source: independent anonymous screenshot review, `docs/design/audit-reports/2026-09-03-brand-breakthrough/evidence/blind-round-2/`. All four winning options were the implementation candidates; overall scores were brand recognition 4.5, memorability 4.5, hierarchy 4.4, readability 4.2, and mobile completion 4.4.

### Health-events triage — care relay stage

- Source: `docs/design/audit-reports/2026-09-03-brand-breakthrough/evidence/after/*-home-triage.png`; populated triage state at 375×667, 390×844, 430×932, and 1280×800.
- Reuse: person, nurse, and record action as three meaningful anchors on one continuous path; a 5:7 asymmetric stage on wide screens.
- Do not copy mechanically: nurse imagery, the wide stage, and the path role belong to care hand-off and recording contexts.

### Health-events list — continuous narrative rail

- Source: `docs/design/audit-reports/2026-09-03-brand-breakthrough/evidence/after/*-health-events-list.png`; populated list state at all four audit viewports.
- Reuse: open chronological bands, dates as beats, and a rail that encodes order and focus only.
- Do not copy mechanically: the rail must not imply medical severity or merge separate events into one episode; keep independent row affordances.

### Health profile — personal health dossier

- Source: `docs/design/audit-reports/2026-09-03-brand-breakthrough/evidence/after/*-health-profile.png`; default profile directory at all four audit viewports.
- Reuse: important longitudinal facts before utility search, one dossier spine, and visibly distinct priority and directory chapters.
- Do not copy mechanically: search-first catalogues and focused forms should retain their own task hierarchy.

### Family — care relationship index

- Source: `docs/design/audit-reports/2026-09-03-brand-breakthrough/evidence/after/*-family.png`; populated current-person state at all four audit viewports.
- Reuse: the current cared-for person as the focal anchor, other people as open rows, and one relationship bond.
- Do not copy mechanically: never infer a family tree, hierarchy, or relationship the data does not contain; do not tighten the iPhone SE bond-to-row spacing.
