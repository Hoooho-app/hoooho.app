# Hoooho product constitution

## Identity and purpose

The brand is written **Hoooho**: one capital `H`, followed by lowercase letters. User-visible variants such as `Hoho`, `HOHO`, and `HOOOHO` are defects unless they are historical quotations. Persistence keys and protocol identifiers are not renamed by visual work.

Hoooho is an online triage desk and pre-visit front desk. It helps people with initial routing, gradual health-story organization, and a concise consultation summary that a clinician or AI can understand quickly. It is not a doctor and does not replace diagnosis or prescribing.

The experience should communicate that users do not need to speak perfectly, know medical vocabulary, or organize everything first. They can record and supplement information over time, arrive at care less anxious, and reduce omissions.

## Frozen product structure

Visual and interaction governance does not change product positioning, first-level pages, navigation, core flows, APIs, schemas, field meaning, approved copy, frozen modules, nurse character, original motion/video assets, or unrelated features. “健康随记” and other confirmed product names remain unchanged.

## Presentation authority

Inside the frozen business boundary, design-quality work may improve internal hierarchy, composition, density, whitespace, alignment, type scale, component treatment, action hierarchy, icons, semantic color balance, borders, radii, shadows, overlays, focus, feedback, motion timing, responsive behavior, touch ergonomics, shared components, and semantic tokens.

The operating principle is: **business structure is frozen; presentation structure may mature**. Presentation changes must improve clarity, trust, accessibility, and completion speed rather than merely look different.

## Decision boundary

Professional implementation details inside an explicitly approved visual direction remain with the design-system engineer. Escalate when a change would alter a product capability, first-level page, navigation, business step, approved copy, stored-data meaning, backend contract, irreversible data, major production dependency, fundamental brand strategy, or a visual direction that lacks explicit product-owner acceptance.
