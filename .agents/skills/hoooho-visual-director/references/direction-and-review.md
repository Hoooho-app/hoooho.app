# Direction and review method

## Hoooho-specific thesis

Hoooho is an online triage desk and pre-visit front desk—not a doctor or medical-record back office. Its interface should help someone record incomplete information calmly, add detail over time, and arrive at care prepared.

The visual signature is the restrained three-point continuous health trace already defined by Design System V2. Spend distinctiveness there and in information rhythm; keep surrounding surfaces quiet and precise.

## Direction comparison

For A, B, and C record: visual thesis, token impact, component impact, representative page treatment, accessibility risk, implementation risk, and why it is or is not suitable for release. A and B must reuse the current Design System. C is a critique artifact unless separately authorized.

## Evidence rules

- Use stable viewport, data, route, scroll position, and browser state for before/after images.
- Identify the intended first visual focus and verify it is actually dominant.
- Treat white space, dividers, labels, and motion as information structure, not decoration.
- Check that one primary action leads each view and that secondary actions remain legible without competing.
- Verify Chinese text wrapping, long values, numeric alignment, and 320–430 px behavior.
- Record regressions and rejected proposals in the design sample library.

## Rejection triggers

Reject a direction when it relies mainly on recoloring, stronger shadows, larger radii, larger headings, or extra whitespace; resembles a generic AI/SaaS template; makes a mobile page feel like a compressed desktop surface; reduces readability or task completion; fragments the visual language; or damages approved character, motion, or media assets.
